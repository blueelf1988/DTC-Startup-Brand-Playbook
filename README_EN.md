## What Is This?

When you have a DTC brand startup idea, you need quick answers to critical questions:

- Is this worth pursuing? (GO / NO-GO)
- Where's the market window? (Opportunity Analysis)
- How do we win? (Brand Strategy + Competitive Moat)
- How do we make money? (Business Model + Unit Economics)
- How do we execute? (GTM Strategy + 12-Month Roadmap)

**DTC Startup Brand Playbook** is a battle-tested methodology that answers all of these systematically — in a format that investors, partners, and your own team can act on immediately.

---

## Core Design Philosophy

```
Positioning → GO/NO-GO → Opportunity → Brand Strategy → Business Model → GTM → Roadmap → Risk & Org
     ↓           ↓           ↓              ↓              ↓           ↓       ↓           ↓
  Define      Verdict     Why Now?      How to Win?    How to $?    How to    12-Month   Team +
  the space   first                                          Execute   Plan      Risk
```

### Three Principles

1. **Verdict First, Evidence Second** — Open with positioning + GO/NO-GO judgment. No fluff.
2. **Why Now → How to Win → How to Execute** — Standard strategy consulting narrative arc
3. **Data-Driven, Insight-Closed** — Every module presented as a data table, closed with one sharp insight

## Template Structure (7 Modules)

| # | Module | Core Question | Key Output |
|---|--------|--------------|------------|
| — | **Business Positioning** | What are we? What are we NOT? | One-liner + category definition |
| — | **GO/NO-GO Verdict** | Is this worth doing? | 5-dimension assessment + strategic discipline |
| 01 | **Opportunity Analysis** | Why now? | 3 market signals + 4-quadrant competitive map + TAM/SAM/SOM |
| 02 | **Brand Strategy** | How do we win? | STP + moat design + product pyramid + pricing |
| 03 | **Business Model** | How do we make money? | Revenue flywheel + Unit Economics vs. industry benchmarks |
| 04 | **GTM Strategy** | How do we execute? | 5P framework (Product/Price/Place/Promotion/Packaging) |
| 05 | **12-Month Roadmap** | What's the plan? | MVP phase (4mo) + Growth phase (8mo) + ROAS/MER evolution + hard Go/No-Go gate |
| 06 | **Risk & Organization** | Who does what? What can go wrong? | Risk matrix (probability × impact) + phased team structure |
| — | **Appendix** | Supporting data | Product concept matrix + data sources |

## Quality Checklist

After generating a playbook, verify:

- [ ] Business positioning is crystal clear (what we ARE vs. what we are NOT)
- [ ] GO/NO-GO includes explicit verdict AND strategic discipline
- [ ] Every major module has at least one data-backed insight
- [ ] Competitor benchmarking includes concrete data (revenue/funding/scale)
- [ ] Unit Economics include industry benchmark comparison
- [ ] MVP phase has a hard Go/No-Go decision gate (≥4 quantified metrics)
- [ ] ROAS/MER evolution is projected across phases
- [ ] Risk matrix includes probability AND impact assessment
- [ ] Team structure is phased (MVP → Growth → Scale)
- [ ] Appendix cites all data sources
